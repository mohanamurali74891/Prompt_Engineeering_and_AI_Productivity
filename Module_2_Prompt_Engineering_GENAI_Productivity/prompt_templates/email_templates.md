## Template 1: Professional Cold Outreach Email

```
ROLE: You are an expert business communication specialist.

CONTEXT: I need to write a cold outreach email to [TARGET_PERSON] at [COMPANY_NAME].
My name is [YOUR_NAME] and I work at [YOUR_COMPANY/ROLE].

INSTRUCTIONS:
- Write a concise, professional cold outreach email
- Subject line should be compelling and under 60 characters
- Body should be under 150 words
- Include a clear call to action
- Tone: professional yet personable

CONSTRAINTS:
- Do NOT use generic openers like "I hope this email finds you well"
- Do NOT make the email about yourself — focus on value for the recipient
- Format: Subject line first, then body

INPUT VARIABLES:
- Target Person: [NAME]
- Their Role: [ROLE]
- Company: [COMPANY]
- My Goal: [GOAL — e.g., request a meeting, partnership, interview]
- Key Value Proposition: [WHAT I OFFER THEM]

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Subject: [subject line]

[email body]

[signature]
```

**Example Usage:**
```
Target Person: Priya Sharma
Their Role: Head of AI Research
Company: TechVision Labs
My Goal: Request a 30-minute informational interview
Key Value Proposition: I am a Gen AI intern with research on LLM prompt optimization
```

## Template 2: Follow-Up Email

```
ROLE: You are a professional communication coach.

CONTEXT: I previously sent an email to [NAME] about [TOPIC] on [DATE].
I haven't received a response and want to follow up politely.

INSTRUCTIONS:
- Write a brief, non-pushy follow-up email
- Reference the previous email naturally
- Add new value or a reason for them to respond
- Keep it under 100 words
- End with a simple, low-friction call to action

CONSTRAINTS:
- Do NOT sound desperate or impatient
- Do NOT just say "following up on my previous email"
- Be specific and helpful

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Subject: Re: [original subject]

[email body]
```

## Template 3: Internship / Job Application Email

```
ROLE: You are a career coach helping a student apply for internships.

CONTEXT:
- Student Name: [NAME]
- Applying for: [ROLE] at [COMPANY]
- Background: [SKILLS/EXPERIENCE in 1-2 lines]
- Why this company: [SPECIFIC REASON]

INSTRUCTIONS:
- Write a compelling application email
- Subject line should mention the role clearly
- First paragraph: hook with a specific achievement or passion
- Second paragraph: why this company specifically
- Third paragraph: what you bring + CTA
- Keep under 200 words

CONSTRAINTS:
- Be specific, not generic
- No clichés like "I am a hardworking individual"
- Attach resume note at the end

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Subject: [subject]

[3-paragraph body]

Best regards,
[Name]
P.S. Resume attached for your reference.
```

## Template 4: Thank You / Appreciation Email

```
ROLE: Professional email writer

CONTEXT: I want to send a thank-you email to [PERSON] for [REASON].

INSTRUCTIONS:
- Be genuine and specific (reference what they did)
- Keep it brief — under 80 words
- End with a forward-looking statement

OUTPUT:
Subject: Thank You — [specific topic]

[body]
```

## Template 5: Meeting Request Email

```
ROLE: Executive assistant helping schedule meetings

CONTEXT:
- Requestor: [YOUR NAME]
- Recipient: [NAME, ROLE]
- Purpose: [MEETING GOAL]
- Preferred times: [TIME SLOTS]
- Duration: [30 min / 1 hour]

INSTRUCTIONS:
- Write a polite meeting request email
- State the agenda clearly
- Offer flexible time slots
- Include a calendar link placeholder

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Subject: Meeting Request — [Topic]

[body with agenda + time slots]
```
